# BMI Calculator

A simple tool to calculate your Body Mass Index. Enter your name, height, and weight to get your BMI and health insights.

## How to Use

1. Open 'index.html' in your browser
2. Enter your name
3. Enter your height in centimeters
4. Enter your weight in kilograms
5. Click "Calculate my BMI"
6. See your result with health guidance

## Files

- 'index.html' - The app
- 'style.css' - Design and layout
- 'script.js' - Does the calculation
- 'assets/' - Images

## How It Works

The calculator uses your height and weight to compute your BMI using this formula:

**BMI = weight (kg) ÷ height (m)²**

Results are color-coded:
- Blue = Underweight
- Green = Healthy
- Yellow = Overweight
- Red = Obese

No installation needed. Just open and use.
